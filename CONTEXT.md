# Podracer

A browser podracing game: racers fly pods over tracks on several planets. This glossary fixes the words for pods, their look, their damage and the ground they fly over.

## Language

### Racer

**Pod**:
The vehicle: two or more engines, the energy binder between them, the control cables and one cockpit.
_Avoid_: Podracer (that is the game), ship, car

**Racer**:
One pod together with its pilot. There are 14.
_Avoid_: Fahrer, character, pod (when the pilot is meant too)

**Cockpit**:
The gondola the pilot sits in; it hangs on the cables behind the engines.
_Avoid_: Kanzel (that is only the glass), capsule, cabin

**Gegner**:
A racer flown by the AI.
_Avoid_: Rival, Opponent (in UI text), Bot

### Look

**Lackierung**:
A pod's colours, pattern and finish.
_Avoid_: Skin, Livery (in UI text), Design

**Tuning**:
The seven stat steps (0–5) of a pod.
_Avoid_: Upgrade, Aufwertung

**Tuning-Teil**:
A visible part on the pod that follows a tuning step.
_Avoid_: Upgrade-Teil

**Style-Teil**:
A freely chosen look part in one of the slots Finnen, Einlass, Düse, Kanzel. It changes no stat.
_Avoid_: Kit, Bodykit, Zubehör

### Damage

**Schadensmodell**:
How one racer takes damage: its Panzerzonen and its Bruchteile.
_Avoid_: Schadensprofil, damage profile (in UI text)

**Panzerzone**:
A direction a hit comes from (front, side, top, belly, heat) together with the racer's toughness there.
_Avoid_: Trefferzone, hitbox

**Bruchteil**:
A part that breaks off when an engine side's health falls through a step. It stays gone until respawn.
_Avoid_: Schadensteil, break part (in UI text)

**Treffermarke**:
A burn and bare-metal mark on the hull where a hit landed.
_Avoid_: Delle, dent

### Track

**Untergrund**:
What the track runs over in one passage: the built deck or a natural ground (sand, rock, snow, ice, mud …). It changes look, grip, top speed and engine heat.
_Avoid_: Belag, Textur (that is only its picture), surface (in UI text)

**Passage**:
A stretch of track with one Untergrund.
_Avoid_: Abschnitt (that is any stretch), Sektor

**Felsgrenze**:
The solid border of a natural passage where no canyon wall stands: one line for the physics, as high as the rocks are tall.
_Avoid_: Felswand (that is a canyon wall), Leitplanke, Bande

**Felsreihe**:
The row of single rocks the player sees along a Felsgrenze.
_Avoid_: Felsband, Felsmauer

**Schulter**:
The ground of a natural road beyond the road edge: it comes down to the terrain and thins out into it.
_Avoid_: Randstreifen, Bankett, Böschung (that is the terrain's own slope)

**Verwehung**:
Sand or snow the wind has blown against a border; it thins out towards the middle of the road.
_Avoid_: Düne (that is an Untergrund), Schneewehe

**Schneedecke**:
The layer of loose snow on a powder snow field. Pods press trails into it; a firm trail is faster.
_Avoid_: Schneeschicht, Pulverschnee (that is the Untergrund)
