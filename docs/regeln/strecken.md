# Rules: tracks, tunnels, landscape

Part of the agent manual (`CLAUDE.md`). Read this before you change how a track is built: layout, surfaces, tunnels, caves, crossings, set pieces, dressing, landscape and decor.

- The tag at the end of a rule is the session that wrote it; rules written after Session 20 carry their date. **If two rules disagree, the later one wins** (date > `S20 …` > `S19` > … > `≤ S9`).
- Design intent: `DESIGN.md` → „Strecken & Bauwerke" and „Landschaft & Leben".
- What a pod does in a tunnel or under an overhead (hull, vault, `p.upper`): `physik-steuerung.md`. Textures and photo sets: `grafik.md`.

## Layout, width, profile
- **Track layouts are expanded procedurally** in `trackBuilder.ts → controlPoints()` (scale `TRACK_SCALE`, meander insertion with clearance check, two open flats). `data.ts` control points stay the designer's base layout; index-based hints (`def.shortcuts`) must go through the returned `map`. `NO_EXPAND=1` (CI) builds the old layouts. Fraction-based fields (canyons, ramps, set pieces …) shift slightly – that is expected. *(S18)*
- **Open flats** = samples with `hw > wideLim` (min(1.45 × median, 45 m)): no canyon flag, no tunnels. Keep anything that assumes a narrow road (tunnels, arches) out of them. *(S18)*
- **Track width:** change only `TRACK_WIDTH`; rerun `npm run check`. *(S11)*
- **Jump ramps (`TrackDef.ramps {at, len, h, gap}`, `rampProfile`; 2026-11-25):** 30–40 m long, 2–2.6 m high (they were 4–6 m), eased in over the first 40 % and then straight to the lip – 8–9 % steep there, which at full speed is the upward speed `launchVy` gives every pod (no kink in the flight path). Never convex: where a ramp flattens towards the lip a fast pod lifts off before it. **Do not change `len` lightly:** tunnels, obstacles, surfaces and set pieces are placed around the ramp samples – ramps twice as long moved tunnels on six tracks (compare `node tools/ci/out/info.mjs <track>` before and after: it must not differ).
- **Track profile (`trackBuilder.ts`):** banking via `finalizeSamples(S, closed, banked = true)` for the main path only; hills are added after ramps are placed and are damped to 0 at grid/finish/ramps/gaps/zero-G and wherever the track comes within `hwA + hwB + 14 m` of itself. Do not add height elsewhere (jump tuning + over/underpass clearance depend on it). *(S10)*

## Surfaces and flat basins
- **Surfaces (`surfaces.ts`, `Sample.surf`):** every sample carries an index into `SURFACES` (0 = built race deck). The file is pure data (no three/DOM imports – builder, physics, CI and world share it). New surface → entry in `SURFACES` (grip / vmax / heat, photo set + procedural fallback kind + tile + palette, wake) and in `THEME_SURFACES` (`general` / `open` / `cave` per theme, `pal` overrides). `buildTrack` assigns them in two places: the open runs (`openRuns`, before `finalizeSamples`) and all remaining passages (end of `buildTrack`). Global `NO_SURFACES` = old uniform deck for A/B. *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Flat basins (`Sample.lake`, `lakeY`, `lakeL` / `lakeR`, `lvl`)** = lakes, frozen lakes, plateaus (`SurfaceDef.flat`). Profile and camber are levelled over `lvl`; rails, kerbs, `trackDetail`, `props`, `scifi`, `mechProps` all skip `lake` samples; the edge is marked by beacons. `world.ts → buildBasins` draws one level sheet per run (liquid → `waterMaterial(…, LIQUIDS[liquid])` / `lavaSeaMaterial`, else the surface material). **The sheet is double-wound with +Y normals** because it folds over itself wherever `hw + shore > R`; keep it level and textured in world space so overlaps stay invisible. Where the road band itself is wider than the corner radius (`hw · |curv| ≥ 1`) the inner shore keeps 34 m so the sheets before and after the kink overlap – otherwise a diamond-shaped hole opens. Aquilaris lakes are the ocean itself (`seaY0 + 0.4`, no sheet). `tools/ci/info.ts` prints `fold` and `basin` lines for these cases. *(S20 Strecken)*
- **No road may be wider than its bend allows (2026-11-23): half width ≤ 0.85 × curve radius, checked for all 13 tracks** (unit check `layout: the road never folds …`, limit 0.9). Beyond 1.0 the inner edge runs backwards and the ribbon lies over itself – overlapping road mesh, a folded border line (rocks, rails, beacons have nowhere to stand). `buildTrack` rounds such bends right after the centre-line smoothing and before the samples are re-spaced: the centre line is smoothed only around the offending samples (mask: full within 20 samples, fading over the next 30; ±12-sample triangle per step; the samples are spread evenly again after every step – without that they bunch up in the bend and the smoothing stalls) until the limit holds. The road keeps its width; the corner becomes a sweeping curve. Tracks without such a bend stay bit-identical. Changed by it: Boonta (the 121° hairpin before the plateau, radius 20 → 65 m, and the dune corner; lap 9.63 → 9.40 km), Mos Espa, Aquilaris, marginally Dünenpass, Kristall and Frostspalt. **Side effect to know:** surfaces and open flats are laid out after the geometry – on Boonta the plateau now covers the rounded corner (samples 3267–3699 instead of 3494–3727) and all passages shifted. **Do not** fix a fold by moving the centre line outwards and narrowing the road on the inside (tried first): the sideways shift has to run in and out, and on a road 108 m wide those S-bends folded the road again a hundred metres further on. *(2026-11-23)*
- **Basins inside a tight bend (`lakeL` / `lakeR` limit in `buildTrack`):** the sheet of a basin may reach `radius − half width − 12 m` beyond the inner road edge (further in, its cross-sections cross). Where that leaves less than 30 m (plateau) or 12 m (lake), the shore is set to 34 m instead: the sheets before and after the apex then overlap past the centre of the bend (level, double-wound, world-space UVs → one surface). Without it a rock-lined pit opened between the beacons and the centre of the bend – seen on the Boonta plateau the moment its fold was rounded (half width 54 m, radius 65 m: 11 m left, shore 0). A lake keeps its bank inside the bend from 12 m on. *(2026-11-23)*
- **Zero-G tubes (2026-11-23):** flat inside (`bank` 0). The road loses its bank over `ZG_UNBANK` 30 samples before a tube and gets it back behind it (`finalizeSamples`) – before, a road banked at up to 21° met the flat tube in one sample, a step of metres at the road edge that threw a pod leaving the tube off-centre into the air. **Boulders:** `buildZgRocks` at the end of `buildTrack` puts one every 150–200 m into every tube of the main path, none within 80 m of an end; each swings slowly (≤ 2.5 m/s) about a point within 9 m of the axis and never reaches the wall. Its lowest point stays ≥ 1.2 m above the deck (2026-11-25: boulders placed up to 12 m below the axis sat in or under the road). It uses its own hash, not the builder's random sequence (adding them must not change anything else on the track). The mesh (`setPieces.ts`, instanced) has its own material: plain grey with a fresnel rim in `theme.barrierColor` – the tubes stand in the dark and a boulder without the rim is black on black. The warning sign at a tube's mouth faces the arriving pods (it used to show its back: mirror writing).
- **Tracks in loose ground (`groundTracks.ts`, 2026-11-23, user: "dezente Fahrspuren auch im Sand / Mondstaub" – look only):** `trackLife(surf)` = seconds a track stays: 40 on sand, dunes, beach, ash (`TRACK_SAND_LIFE`), `Infinity` on moon dust, 0 everywhere else. `TrackLayer` (pure) is a ring of quads; `step(pods, td, time)` lays one quad per engine bank every `TRACK_SEG` m (dunes 3 m – curved crests) for every pod that is grounded close over such ground, at `roadSurfaceAt + TRACK_LIFT` (the ground layer of the world lies up to 0.10 m above the physical road). **The newest quad of a stripe grows with the pod (2026-11-25, user: the own tracks were not seen being made at racing speed in the chase view):** it is started `TRACK_MIN` 0.2 m past the last cross-section and its front edge moves to the engines in every frame (`front`); `TRACK_SEG` m long, the next one starts there. Every corner carries the time the pod was there: the stripe fades in from the engines backwards over `TRACK_FADE_IN` 0.04 s (5 m at 120 m/s; it was 0.4 s per quad = 48 m, behind the chase camera). All four corners of a quad run out at the same moment (the front ones get a shorter life) – the mesh collapses a quad that has run out. **A cell of the road gets no second stripe** while one lies there or in the neighbouring cell across (`claim`: cells of 4 m × `TRACK_BUCKET`, fresh = younger than ¾ of its life; moon dust: for ever) – without it the racing line turns darker with every pod and the ring fills up in one lap. A jump in position (reset) or a gap starts a new stripe. `buildGroundTracks` shows the ring with one mesh (`renderOrder −1`, right after the ground): black, transparent, a flat band with a darker line at each edge, strength per surface (`STRENGTH`: ash 1.8, moon dust 2.2 – on dark ground 10 % darker is invisible), fading over the second half of its life; a quad whose life is over collapses to a point. The ring is primed with one invisible quad (the pipeline compiles while loading; WebGPU warns about a draw call with 0 indices). `Game` steps the layer only in the states `race` and `post`. Ring: 36 000 quads, 72 000 on a track with moon dust. Unit checks `tracks: …` (6). A new loose surface: one line in `LIFE` (and `STRENGTH` if it is dark).
- **3D snow on the powder fields (`snowField.ts`, pure; `snowDeck.ts`; 2026-11-23, user: "dynamische Höhe 1–3 m"):** every contiguous run of `powder` samples gets a grid of 1 m cells in strip coordinates (`SnowRun`: `nx` across, `nz` along) with TWO bytes per cell. `depth` = height of the fresh drift (0 … `SNOW_MAX` = 3 m), fixed for the race: `driftHeight` (ridges turned 25° against the track, sharp crest lines), kept shallow (`SNOW_SHALLOW`) over `SNOW_LEAD` samples at both ends, within `SNOW_CLEAR_PIECE` samples of a set piece (the gate) and within `SNOW_CLEAR_ROCK` m of an obstacle – nothing may be buried; a new thing placed on a powder field needs the same. `data` = how much is cut away (0 … 255 = a trench down to `SNOW_FLOOR`). `heightAt` = what stands now. Row `j` lies at sample coordinate `(j − 0.5) / SNOW_SUB` – `read`, `stamp`, the drift generator and the mesh all use this. `SnowField` hangs on `TrackData.snow` and exists only while a race runs (`Game.start`, the node sim, a unit check); without it powder is plain powder. `stepPod` cuts with two discs at the NOSE of the engines (`podParams().nose`), several times per step (`STAMP_EVERY`), amount 1: one pass = a finished trench. Slower than 25 m/s it clears a pit under the whole pod (reset, crash). Snow squalls call `snowIn` (`Game.update`). The world draws one layer per run (`buildSnowDeck`): both arrays in ONE two-channel texture (R cut, G drift), the vertex shader lifts the mesh to `heightAt` (the cut sharpened by a smoothstep, a shoulder beside a fresh cut), the fragment shader takes slope, hollow/crest and depth below the drift from the same texture. Unit checks `snow: …` (5). *(2026-11-23; the step before, 2026-11-22: 0.58 m with pressed trails)*
- **Open sections – a rock border is one line for the physics and a row of rocks for the eye** (user decision 2026-11-22; rows of single rocks since 2026-11-23). The line: at the end of `buildTrack` every side of a main-road sample over natural ground (`surf > 0`, not the station `deck`; no basin, tunnel, bridge, underpass, ramp, gap, zero-G, fork mouth, existing canyon wall) gets `hL` / `hR` = 2.6 … 7 m (outcrops up to 12 m) and the flag `rockL` / `rockR`. Physics, Gegner and checks read only that: solid at `wallL` / `wallR` = `hw − RAIL_INSET` (where the rail stood – gates, arches and gantries are built for that width) up to the height `hL` / `hR`, soft above (`soft` in `stepPod`). A run of border samples comes down to 1.2 m at both ends unless it joins a canyon wall. Built decks keep their rails, basins their beacons. `NO_ROCK_BORDERS` (global) = the rails of before. What the player sees is made from the line in three places – change them together:
  1. `borderRocks.ts` (pure data, runs in node): one rock after the other along the line. A rock touches the line with its face (at most `ROCK_PROTRUDE` = 0.25 m inside it), overlaps its neighbour (`ROCK_OVERLAP`), is 0.82 – 1.12 × the border height tall; about every third rock has a smaller one behind it. The row is laid out along the border LINE, so on the inside of a bend a rock that touches the line in one place cuts it in the next: `intrusion()` measures the rock in the frame of every sample it reaches and pushes it back; where that does not help the rock is left out (that was the case where a road folded over itself; since 2026-11-23 no road does – rule "bend rounding" below, unit check `layout: …`). A rock is as tall as the border AT THE PLACE WHERE IT ENDS UP: its size sets its distance from the rock before it, so the place is estimated first and corrected twice (until 2026-11-23 the estimate was final – where the border height changes quickly a rock stood up to 2 m lower than the border the physics knows; the check `rock rows: a rock is about as tall …` turned red on the first re-roll of the rows).
  2. `world.ts → addBorderRocks`: shape (two boulder shapes, measured reach in 48 directions – the row is laid out with the real shapes), seat (base below the lowest DRAWN ground under the rock, `terrainMeshHeight`; top as high above the road edge as the border), rocks that would reach into another stretch of road (forks, hairpins) are made smaller or dropped. Instanced per 340 m sector with a far twin; material `cliff`, mesh name `borderRocks`.
  3. No wall sheet and no massif behind a rock border (`okW` in `world.ts`, `H` in `terrain.ts → evalWithSample`): the land behind the row stays open. `trackDetail` sets no studs, no wall lamps and no corner kerbs there.
  Unit checks `open sections: …` (the line) and `rock rows: …` (the rocks: none in the road, the row closed, height and overlap). The width varies since 2026-11-23: next rule. *(2026-11-23)*
- **Bays – between two rock borders the road opens and closes again (`Sample.bay`, 2026-11-23; user: "keine fest abgesteckten Strecken").** A rock row that follows the road at one distance is still a fence. `trackBuilder.ts` (block "open sections, the width breathes", right after the rock borders are flagged) adds a bay to `hw` where BOTH sides are rock borders: wide bays by a slow noise (per side up to 0.55 × the half width, at most 18 m, 150–400 m long) and small coves (up to 5 m, every 50–100 m). `Sample.bay` = the extra half width, already contained in `hw`. Rules:
  1. **Only ever wider than designed.** Reference speeds, gates and the racing line stay valid. The rivals' ideal line uses the DESIGNED half width (`physics.ts`: `lineHw = hw − bay`) – with the full width they wove into every bay, up to 10 m sideways within 120 m; evading and overtaking use the bay (`safeLim` reads the real walls, tightest over the next 88 m, so a closing bay is seen in time).
  2. **Limits, each with a reason:** 0 within 8 samples of anything that is not a rock border on both sides (deck, basin, tunnel, bridge, fork mouth, canyon wall – the rock row eases down to a kerb over these 8); at most 0.3 m per sample (15 %, the border turns by 8.5°); half width at most 0.6 × the bend radius (the layout's fold limit is 0.9); the border at most 1.5 m higher or lower than designed (`BAY_TILT`: a banked bend is a tilted plane, bank 0.196 × 18 m of bay = 3.5 m, and where the bank sets in the edge twists by that within 20 m – the 13.75 m terrain mesh of "Niedrig" stood 0.5 m above the road edge on Boonta; `tools/ci/poke.ts` found it, now 0 points on all tracks at 4.5 / 7 / 13.75 m, also with a limit of 2 m; unit check `open sections: the terrain stays below the road in every bay of …`, red without the limit); 24 m between the border of a bay and every other piece of road, two stretches that face each other share the room. "Other piece of road" = a branch, or the main road where it runs more than 60° off or lies more than 5 half widths + 90 m further along (`baySameStretch`) – a fixed 120 m counted the continuation of these 60–90 m wide roads as a neighbour and cut most bays.
  3. **Order matters:** the bays are added after forks, tunnels, basins and surfaces are placed (they need to know what is where) and before the wall offsets, obstacles, boost pads and set pieces (which use the full width). The limits are eroded by 16 samples before slope limit and smoothing, so the smoothing can never lift a sample above its limit.
  4. `NO_BAYS` (global; `NO_BAYS=1` for `sim.mjs`) = one width as before. Unit checks `open sections: the road opens into bays …`, `open sections: a bay keeps its distance …` and `open sections: the terrain stays below the road in every bay of …` (four tracks).
  - Measured: of the 51 km between two rock borders 47 % are more than 2 m wider per side (14 km more than 5 m, 4 km more than 10 m), 2 to 15 bays per track, largest 9 m (Malastare) to 18 m. Rivals over three seeds, bays against none: average speed 109.9 against 110.1 m/s, slowest pod 100.3 against 100.8 m/s, no pod destroyed in either (the scatter between seeds is larger).
  - Not built: the two sides open TOGETHER (one `hw` for both). Independent sides would need `wallL` / `wallR` instead of `hw` in the rivals' line, the road mesh, the terrain corridor and the snow deck. Basins keep their beacons.
- **The edge of a natural road (2026-11-23, user decision: no visible road edge on natural ground):** `world.ts` builds ONE grid per natural passage – left edge strip · road · right edge strip – and cuts it into meshes afterwards, so the normals run smoothly over the road edge. Per sample and side (`edgeStrips`): kind 1 **shoulder** beside a rock border – the ground of the road runs on beyond the edge, follows the plane of the road for a tenth of its width, comes down to the drawn terrain and thins out (alpha) over `SHOULDER` = 9 m + 0.35 mesh cells; eased to 0 over 6 samples at the ends of a border, shortened where another stretch of road is near. Kind 2 **apron** beside a canyon wall – 1.7 m of ground banked up 0.34 m against the wall foot (it covers the rock ditch between road and wall). Heights come from `edgeY`, which asks `terrainMeshHeight` (the terrain MESH: between its vertices the mesh cuts every corner of the field by up to a metre – a strip laid on `heightAt` floats or dives). Branch roads get no apron where they are tucked under the main road (first and last 70 samples). The rock fascia below a natural road (`skirt`) starts 0.16 m lower than below a deck, or its top shows as a line of rock along the sand. *(2026-11-23)*

## Tunnels and caves
- **The end wall of a cave branch tube is CUT along the main cavern, not kept or dropped by whole triangles:** `clipOutside` (`meshClip.ts`, pure) clips the finely divided end disc (`caveEnd`, 6 rings) against `insideCave(…, 0.5)`. One fan of large triangles, kept as soon as one corner lay in the rock, stood in the fork as a flat slab (user report 2026-11-22). `insideCave` reaches no further than 0.2 m past the end of a tube – with 3.2 m the main shell was cut open behind the end wall. The shells keep the whole-triangle rule (`cull`): their triangles are small. *(2026-11-22)*
- **Tunnel cross-section has ONE source:** `tunnelProfile(l)`, `tunnelArc(ang)`, `tunnelCeiling(sample, lat)` in `trackBuilder.ts` (superellipse `TUNNEL_N = 3`, half width `hw + TUNNEL_SHOULDER`). Mesh, ceiling collision, camera clamp and stalactites must use them. Roof constants in `buildTrack`: `STD_ROOF 27 / 30`, `UNDER_ROOF 26 / 28`, `SC_ROOF 25 / 27`, `CAVE_ROOF 27 / 29` (+ chamber rise), `SEA_ROOF 26` (second value = ice). Crossing separation follows `UNDER_ROOF + 11`. *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Caves are one continuous `tun === 3` run.** The road itself widens in the chambers (`caveW` is gone), cave branch tubes are roofed end to end and merged with the main tube by a shell union in `buildTunnel` (`insideCave` + triangle culling). Cliffs are skipped in caves (`world.ts`, `terrain.ts`). Every extra face inside a cave (end walls, wedge floors) needs real texture coordinates – a fan that reuses ring vertices collapses the texture into stripes. *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Tunnels & branching caves:** heights, profile and cave layout are superseded by the rules tagged S20 Strecken (no `caveW` any more). Still valid: caves fork into reuniting `Path.isCave` tubes marked by `buildCaveForkPylons`. Shortcut/cave merges go through `stitchBranch` (banked height match + actual `q.hw` check). *(S19)*
- **Caves** are configured in `CAVES` (theme → [samples, depth, chambers]); roof height varies per sample inside a chamber, pillars come from `cavePillars` as kind-1 obstacles. *(S18)*
- **Tunnels have three kinds** (`Sample.tun`: 1 standard, 2 underwater, 3 cave) and are placed in `buildTrack` BEFORE obstacles / hazards / set pieces (those skip `roof > 0`). Underwater tubes and the cave dip the road (`dip()`); never put a ramp within 520–600 m before them (`rampBefore`). *(S17)*
- **`buildTunnel`** (world.ts) builds per roof run: inner shell, berm or caisson, drivable deck (road material, UV integrated per segment), portal faces, ribs, lamps; underwater: window faces share the shell's vertex buffer and use `oceanWindowMaterial` (scifi.ts) where the tube top is below `td.seaY`. *(S17)*
- **Sea level of ocean worlds is `td.seaY`** (fixed before the dips). The water plane takes a hole mask (`tunnelWaterMask`) where an underwater tube breaks the surface. *(S17)*
- **Tunnels:** `sample.roof > 0` (main path: placed at the end of `buildTrack`, shortcuts: middle half). `buildTunnel` builds one shell per roof run, sheared with `bank`. Physics ceiling, rain cover, lamp strips follow `roof` automatically. *(S16)*

## Crossings, banked decks, overheads
- **Anything under a banked deck follows the camber** (`addViaductPiers`: tilted hammerhead, per-leg height). A level beam sticks out of the low side of a 21° corner. *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Self-crossings (`td.crossings`, `Sample.bridge / underpass`, `tun = 4`):** detected & grade-separated (≥ 30–33.5 m) in `buildTrack`; `terrain.ts` skips `s.bridge` for ground shaping (keeps it in `roadClear`); `buildBridges` renders box girders, abutments and clearance-checked piers. Branch rejoin queries must stay scoped (`nearestMainScoped`). *(S19)*
- **Everything that spans the road is solid:** push an `Overhead` (`{ idx, y0, y1, half }` or `arch: { R, sy, inner?, yOff?, thick?, sp? }`) to `td.overheads` when building it (gantries, start portal, checkpoint gates, rock arches, set-piece arches). Never place such structures on samples with `roof > 0` or `zeroG`. *(S16)*
- **Overheads** may carry `arch: { R, sy }` (checkpoint gates): ceiling = `sy·√((R−1.7)² − lat²)` above the road. *(S15)*
- **Overheads:** `td.overheads` (filled by `trackDetail` gantries and the start portal) are solid in `stepPod`. New overhead structure → push `{ idx, y0, y1, half }`. Keep banner undersides ≥ 16 m above the road. *(S14)*

## Obstacles and set pieces
- **Grounded plates & arches, airlocks:** conform road decals/pads/lips/vents to 3D slope + bank + crown; sink all arch/gate/gantry/mast feet into `heightAt` (or deck cantilevers on `prison`/`ocean`). `gate` set-piece is a 3-bay biome-fused futuristic blast-door airlock (`LIFT = 12.6 m`). *(S19)*
- **Set-piece materials are opaque.** Broken-out arch/ice blocks are capped torus segments; flying chunks are real meshes (`debris.ts → DebrisPool`), not sprites. *(S15)*
- **Breakable / vanished obstacles:** `o.breakable` (crates, rocks r < 2.9). A broken or despawned obstacle gets `broken = true` and is moved to x/z = 1e9, lat = 1e6 – every consumer ignores it without extra checks. Visual removal: `world.breakObstacle(o)`. *(S14)*
- **Obstacles & set pieces (`trackBuilder.ts`, `setPieces.ts`, `world.ts`):** ~7.5 spaced obstacles/track (`≥ 52 m` apart, clear of ramps/landings) marked with glowing ground rings + top strobe beacons; 6 `SetPiece` types (`rotor`, `gate`, `arch`, `icefall`, `boulder`, `bridge`); corridor AI (`aiInputs`) evaluates 27+ lanes with exact ETA timing. *(≤ S9)*

## Dressing: sci-fi, mechanical props, start gate
- **Sci-fi dressing** lives in `scifi.ts` (`SCIFI_THEMES`); edge light strips also light every tunnel. Animated in the shader only. *(S16)*
- **Mechanical props (`mechProps.ts`):** instanced only, no shadows; animated parts via per-frame instance matrices (≤ ~70). Towers stand `wall + 10.5 m` outside the road and are skipped at cliffs, gaps, tunnels, water. *(S11)*
- Start gate: add parts only through `put(mat, geo)` / `strut(...)` in `mkGate` (5 merged buckets). *(S9)*

## Track mesh
- **Texture coordinates along the track must be integrated per segment** (`vAcc += ds / tile(width)`), never `distance / tile(width)` – the latter smears the texture wherever the width changes (worse the further along the track). *(S15)*

## Worlds
- **Oovo IV has ground** (`terrain.ts` prison = moon surface with craters, `LAYERS.oovo`); piers only under built decks; gas giant and ring in `world.ts`; theme sun 19°. *(S20 Strecken)*

## Terrain beside roads, canyon walls, forks
- **Two stretches of road vote on the ground between them, the lower ground wins** (`evalMulti` in `terrain.ts`; a stretch = another path, or the same path more than 200 m further on: fork, reunion, hairpin). `near2` finds the nearest sample of two stretches; the stretch whose road edge is further away is handicapped by `SLOPE` m per metre beyond `R_IN`, so its vote fades without a step. Never judge the ground from the nearest sample alone: the massif of one road then stands on the other road. *(2026-11-22)*
- **The terrain mesh is coarse (4.5 / 7 / 13.75 m cells: `terrainCell(geoQ)`) – every rule that keeps ground off the road must hold for the mesh, not only for the field:** the massif behind a canyon wall starts 1.5 cells behind the wall sheet (`meshCell` parameter of `makeTerrainField`, `dm`), and the ground beside a road lies deeper on a grade (`1.25 + cell · 1.1 · grade`). Measure with `tools/ci/poke.ts` (all tracks, any cell size; before the fix 8 635 points of terrain above the road at 7 m cells, after it 82 – those were the folds of the layout; since the bend rounding of 2026-11-23: 0 at 4.5, 7 and 13.75 m cells, with the bays). *(2026-11-22)*
- **A bridge deck is a ceiling for the ground (2026-11-23).** Bridge samples take no part in shaping the terrain (`grid` in `terrain.ts` skips them – the ground belongs to the road below), so near a bridge the ground is judged from the samples before and behind it alone, and whatever stands beside THEIR road wraps around the end of that stretch onto the deck. On the Methan-Derby a canyon runs straight into two bridges: the massif behind its wall stood across the right half of both decks, up to 18 m high (reported by the user as "Felswände ragen halb auf die Strecke"). Two rules since then: (1) `underBridges` in `terrain.ts` – under a deck and for 2 m + 1.5 mesh cells beside it the ground stays 1.5 m below the low edge of the deck, further out the ceiling rises by 1.2 m per metre; (2) `trackBuilder.ts` – a canyon wall comes down to its 1.2 m rock kerb over the 16 samples before a bridge and rises again behind it, exactly as beside a fork mouth (before: 28 m wall up to the last sample). Unit checks `bridges: …` (terrain mesh of 4.5 / 7 / 13.75 m on every track with a bridge; rule 1 alone is needed – with rule 2 only the ground still stood 5 m above the deck). `tools/ci/poke.ts` skipped bridge samples until that day – it tests them now.
- **A canyon wall is a closed rock body:** the wall sheet (`cliffProf`, `K` points) gets a cap of two more points from its rim back to the ground behind (`KK = K + 2` in `world.ts`), shortened where it would reach over another road (`field.edgeDist`). Crags and ribs scale with the wall height (`lowK`), a low wall is not a row of teeth. The main road's wall comes down to a 1.2 m kerb over the 16 samples before a fork mouth (`trackBuilder.ts`, as `applyShortcutWalls` does for branch roads) – never to 0, or guard rails would appear on those samples. *(2026-11-22)*
- **The harness finds meshes by material name:** `road`, `cliff`, `terrain` (`world.ts`). Hide one of them or draw the terrain as wireframe to see which mesh a defect belongs to – recipe in `tests.md` → „Which mesh is it?". *(2026-11-22)*

## Landscape and decor
- **Mesas have no far twin – measured, not forgotten** (Boonta and Dünenpass, preset "Hoch"): 74–80 mesas × 1 008 triangles are 2.5–2.9 % of a frame (2.8–3.0 M triangles, 161 draw calls). A twin beyond 900 m would save 1.6–1.9 % of the triangles, but the sector chunking a twin needs turns their 2 draw calls into up to 42–46. `mesaGeometry` sets `geometry.name = 'mesa'`; `place()` leaves at once when its count is 0 (the geometry and its twin are disposed). *(2026-11-22)*
- **HQ decor:** `snowPineGeometry` + `pineMaterial` (atlas layout of the tree atlas; alpha in a separate `alphaMap`), `boulderGeometry` + `heightSampler` (CPU displacement by the rock texture's height channel, box-projected uv, creased normals; the LOD twin MUST be `boulderGeometry` at low detail – `rockGeometry` has a different texture mapping and would pop), `cactusGeometry` uv (u = rib phase, v = length / 0.42) + `cactusMaterial`. Builders that the harness should find set `geometry.name` (`boulder`, `cactus`). *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Tree material / atlas:** `foliage.ts → treeMaterial` (map + `alphaMap` + `alphaTest`; alpha must be a separate grey texture – the shadow pass only copies `alphaMap`). UV conventions in `decorGeo.ts`: `barkUV` (left half, mirror-tiled), `plainUV` (white strip), `LEAF_TILE(q)` (4 sprig tiles). Every part merged into a tree needs position, normal, uv, color. *(S18)*
- **Decor density:** `dens` in `scatterDecor` (track length + 20 %, cap 2.6) multiplies every `place()` count – change counts there, not per call. *(S18)*
- **Placing objects on terrain:** use the minimum of several height samples under the footprint (`groundMin` in `world.ts`, `place()` in `scatterDecor`), not the centre height. *(S15)*
- **Decor LOD:** `place(geo, …, { lod: { geo, dist } })` builds a far twin per sector (forces sector chunking); LOD geometry must come from the same seed (`jungleTreeGeometry(seed, gq, true)`, `spireGeometry(seed, halfRadial, sameStrata)`). *(S10)*
- All scatter decor geometry lives in `src/game/decorGeo.ts` (pure functions, seeded, **no materials**). `world.ts → scatterDecor` only calls builders and `place(...)`. *(S9)*
- Variants = call the builder with different seeds and split the instance count (rocks 3, spires 3, mesas 2, trees 2). Keep the total count unchanged. *(S9)*
- Helpers: `ringSurface` (welded lathe grid, outward normals), `tube`, `paint` (vertex colours), `flat` / `smooth`, `merge`. Merged parts need identical attributes (`position, normal, uv` + `color` for trees/pines). *(S9)*
- Keep unit sizes: spire/mesa height `0..1` (scaled by `scaleY`), tree ≈ 19 m, pine ≈ 11 m, crystal cluster `h` param. `foot` in `place` must cover the base radius. *(S9)*
- Triangle budget per instance (gq 2): tree ≤ 1 400, spire ≤ 1 100, mesa ≤ 1 050, snow pine ≤ 750 (cone twin beyond 230 m), boulder 720 within 120 m (980 at gq 3) and 180 beyond, cactus ≤ 600. Check with a node script before raising. *(S9)*
- **Landscape (`world.ts`):** `terrain` → `waterfalls` → `landmarks` (`LandmarkFoot[]`) → `smoke` → `groundCover` (non-overlapping dome LOD) → `wildlife` (landmark avoidance, fleeing herds, perching birds, `nearAudio`) → `trackDetail` (reactive energy barrier) → `batcher.build`. *(≤ S9)*
