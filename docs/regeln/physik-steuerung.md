# Rules: flight physics, AI, input

Part of the agent manual (`CLAUDE.md`). Read this before you change `physics.ts`, the AI or the input handling.

- The tag at the end of a rule is the session that wrote it; rules written after Session 20 carry their date. **If two rules disagree, the later one wins** (date > `S20 …` > `S19` > … > `≤ S9`).
- Design intent: `DESIGN.md` → „Flug, Steuerung & Kamera".
- Measuring the hull of a pod (`podHulls.json`): `pods.md`. Tunnel shapes and overheads as they are built: `strecken.md`.

## Hull, tunnel vault, overheads
- **Decided by the user (2026-11-22): a pod counts as high as its largest outfit.** One hull per pod (`podHulls.json`, "largest outfit"): Teile never change what fits under a vault, no Teil ever sticks in the ceiling; the price is up to 0.70 m less room than the factory pod shows (Aurora XR-7). Do not measure hulls per mounted outfit. *(2026-11-22)*
- **What a pod must stay out of is tested against ONE shape: its hull** – `src/game/podHulls.json` → `podEnvelope().hull` (three parts: left engine side, right engine side, cockpit; slabs `HULL_SLAB` = 3 m wide; the pod in its largest outfit, so Teile never change how a pod drives). `hullWorld(p, env)` in `physics.ts` puts its corners where the model is drawn: yaw, pitch, roll, engine travel, cockpit sway (`COCKPIT_SWAY` – shared with `podModel.ts`). Tunnel vaults (`vaultOver`: every corner against the shell above THAT corner) and overheads (highest / lowest corner under the structure) use it. Never put a pod height or a box into physics again: 2.15 m for all left tall pods 3.5 m inside the vault, and one height above the pod centre ignored pitch (the nose of a climbing pod was up to 5.96 m higher). *(S20 Strecken)*
- **The vault only stops a pod in the AIR** (`!p.grounded`) and is tested LAST in `stepPod`, when pitch, roll, engine travel and cockpit sway are final. A pod that rides on its repulsors is never pressed down: on knife-edge right beside a wall its upper wing tip stands in the vault's flank, and pressing it down would fight the ground clamp – do not add that back. Where a cave forks the higher shell of the two tubes counts; a corner beyond a portal is under the open sky. **Arches are read at the pod CENTRE's lateral position** (their collision shape is padded for that: `arch.inner`); only the pod's height comes from the hull. *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Portal entry is decided by the vault height at the pod's lateral position** (`p.upper`), and the ceiling pushes down at most 45 m/s. Otherwise a pod that enters above the low side vault is pressed through the headwall (CI: downward teleports). *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Never clamp a pod down to a ceiling it approached from outside.** Entering a roofed run decides once: `PodState.upper` (drive on the deck, ground = road + `roof + TUNNEL_DECK`) or inside (ceiling applies). Everything that asks "is this pod under a roof?" must use `roof > 0 && !p.upper`. CI: deck self-test + `downward teleports` must stay 0. *(S17)*

## Hover, ground clamp, engines
- **Ground clamp is exact per point** (front tip vs. road under the tip, tail vs. road under the tail; roll uses barrel-or-wingtip). Never go back to `max(front, back)` floors – on gradients it lifts pods off the road (`grounded = false`, half steering). Clamp range grows with `knife·halfSpan` and lift. *(S10)*
- **Engines never tilt on their own:** `engPL/engPR` stay `0`, `setEngineMotion(yL, yR, 0, 0, 0)`. Steering = pure vertical heave (outer +1.1 m, inner −0.22 m). Whole-pod pitch only from road slope + pitch key. *(S10)*
- **Hover & turbine articulation (`physics.ts`, `podDesigns.ts`, `podModel.ts`):** `HOVER = 2.85 m`, `MIN_ROAD_CLEAR = 0.68 m`. `roadSurfaceAt` includes exact road crown + curb lip. `stepPod` aligns `pitch` to real longitudinal road slope, drives spring-damped opposite L/R engine heave & pitch (`engYL/engYR/engPL/engPR`, outer turbine rises ~1 m, inner tucks ~0.5 m) with diagonal binder tilt & GPU-flexing control cables (`cableFlexMaterial`), and enforces a 5-point repulsor ground clamp under L/R engines & cockpit (0 road clipping). *(≤ S9)*

- **Jumps (2026-11-25, user: "reduziere die generelle Sprungweite. Diese ist gerade bei Boost zu weit."):** a pod leaves a ramp with the upward speed `launchVy(speed, pitch)` – 16 m/s up to 60 m/s (a slow pod must clear a gap of 50 m), 14 at 90 m/s, 11 from 180 m/s on (`JUMP_VY`, `JUMP_SLOW` / `MID` / `FAST`); nose up +1, nose down −1 (`JUMP_PITCH_*`). `stepPod` sets it in the step in which a pod that was on a ramp sample within the last 0.1 s (`p.rampAt`) leaves the ground. **Before**, the upward speed was the ramp's slope × the pod's speed (hard floor: up to 62 m/s) and depended on the frame the pod left in: 160–310 m at full speed, 250–570 m with boost, 144 m at 60 Hz against 215 m at 120 Hz on the same ramp. **Now** 89–126 m at full speed (0.65–0.92 s in the air), 99–159 m with boost (one ramp on Magma: 186 m, the road falls away behind it), 62–70 m at 60 m/s. Nose up carries `JUMP_GLIDE` 0.25 of gravity (it was 0.5: twice the distance) – 10–40 % further over a gap. Nose down still means 2.15 × gravity: a dive right off the lip comes down after 44–50 m. Ramp shape and height: `strecken.md`. Unit checks `jump: …` (3: every ramp of every track; nose up / down / slow; 60 against 120 Hz). **For the coming rework of the jumps** (gaps of 70–90 m, one wide jump only with boost): 70–90 m needs more than today's reach on the short ramps (Frost: 89 m) – raise `h` or the launch for those ramps; "only with boost" needs a ramp whose launch depends on the boost.
- **A normal landing costs no hull (2026-11-23, user request).** Until then every landing faster than 14 m/s damaged the belly – a plain ramp jump comes down at about 30 m/s and cost 6 points each time. Now `LAND_FREE` = 38 m/s (a free fall of 21 m) is free; only the excess hits (`× 0.9 × sturdy`). Two details in `stepPod`: (1) the sink rate is taken BEFORE the hover spring acts (`vyIn`) – the spring takes about a third off within the step in which the pod enters the hover band, so the old code measured 21.6 for a landing at 30; (2) it is measured against the ground (`groundY − prevGroundY`, clamped to −25 … +12 m/s: a road that falls away softens the landing; +12 at most, so that a step in `groundY` – crown, change of path – never counts as a fall). The `land` event (dust, sound, shake, engine heave) fires as before. Unit check `landing: a normal landing costs nothing …` (30 and 36 m/s free, 55 m/s costs). Jumps and drops are designed to come down below `LAND_FREE`.

## Surfaces, slide, boost
- **Handling per surface (`stepPod`):** `SF = surfaceOf(sample.surf)`; grip and top speed apply only when `grounded && !zeroG`, heat scales boost heat-up and cool-down. Rival reference speeds: `q.vmax *= 0.45 + 0.55 · grip`. World base grip lives in `Game.baseGrip` (ice 0.8, jungle 0.9). Retune → `npm run check` and `GRIP=0.88 npm run check`. HUD: `HudState.surface` / `surfaceFx`. *(S20 Strecken)*
- **Boost overdrive:** `PodState.overT` (0..3 s) in the heat block of `stepPod`; HUD field `overdrive`. *(S17)* Over a very hot surface it burns faster: `overT` and the engine damage advance × `max(1, SurfaceDef.heat / 1.5)` – three times as fast over the lava lake. *(2026-11-23)*
- **Lava lake = an extremely short boost (user, 2026-11-23).** `SURFACES` lavalake `heat` 4.5 (was 1.6): a cold engine reaches the limit in 0.6 … 1.0 s (deck: 2.6 … 4.5 s), the overdrive adds one second instead of three, and cooling over the lake runs at 22 %. The crust stays at 1.3. Rivals never use the overdrive (they stop at 60 … 90 % heat), so they only boost for a moment there. Unit checks `lava:` hold both numbers; the HUD line says „Boost nur ganz kurz" for any surface with `heat ≥ 3`.
- **Slide strength** is `sk` (player) / `sk × 0.45` (AI). Retune grip (`0.035`), return rate (`1.4`), slip limiter (`0.88` rad) together and rerun `npm run check`. *(S15)*
- **Slide** (`physics.ts`): state `slideK` 0..1; all slide effects scale with it (grip, yaw boost, decel, body lean). No hard on/off switches. *(S14)*
- **Slide** = `airbraking && |steer| > 0.25 && grounded`. AI adds brake while sliding too fast; keep that when retuning grip (`0.24`). *(S10)*

## Roll, pitch, knife-edge, zero-G
- **Zero-G (`p.zeroG`; rebuilt 2026-11-23, user: "zentrale mittige Anziehung … wie ein Gummiband"):** a spring-damper holds the pod on the tube's axis (`fr.groundY + ZG_AXIS`), sideways and in height: `ZG_OMEGA` 3.5 rad/s, `ZG_ZETA` 0.6 → released it crosses the axis after 0.8 s and swings 10 % past it. Steering (`p.steer`) and climb / sink (`pitchCtl`) do not turn or thrust: they MOVE THE POINT the spring pulls to, up to `zeroGReach()` = `ZG_REACH` 0.7 × tube radius (less where the pod's width would touch the wall). **Down, the reach ends at the deck (2026-11-25, user: "man kann durch den Boden tauchen"):** the axis is only `ZG_AXIS` 7 m above the road, 70 % of a 30–56 m radius would be 14–32 m below it → `zeroGReach().down` = `ZG_DOWN` (`ZG_AXIS − HOVER − 0.3`): held down, the pod sinks to hover height. `stepPod` also holds it there by force (a pod thrown down stops at `groundY + HOVER − 0.4`, no landing, no damage). **The nose follows the tube:** `stepPod` sets the yaw to the tube's direction at the pod (blended in over the first 0.5 s, `p.zgT`); the body-frame velocity is rotated with it – the pod runs on rails, `vr` is the speed across the tube. The height damper works on the speed RELATIVE to the axis (the tube climbs with the track; damping the absolute speed leaves the pod metres behind on a slope). **Exit:** `zeroGReach` looks `ZG_EXIT_FROM` 2.2 s ahead; from there to `ZG_EXIT_FULL` 0.9 s before the end the target height sinks to hover height and the sideways reach shrinks to the middle 35 % of the road behind the tube (`ZG_EXIT_SIDE`) – a pod held at the wall or ceiling comes out centred, at hover height, without damage. Unchanged: `vmax ×1.28`, thrust ×1.35, no drag, the tube wall bounces. **Rivals:** in the tube `aiInputs` sets `steer = wanted lateral position / zeroGReach().side` (the stick position IS the position); pitch 0. **Boulders** (`td.zgRocks`, `zgRockAt(td, rock, time)` – one source for physics, rivals and the mesh, driven by the race time): `collideObstacle(…, floating)` – a hit is a hit on an obstacle rock; rivals put each boulder into their obstacle list where it will be when they arrive, unless it will be above or below them. The sim prints boulders hit and tube-wall touches. Unit checks `zerog: …` (7). *(S16/17 before: free drift, weak pull in height only, steering turned the pod)*
- **Knife-edge controls:** `stepPod` mixes steer/pitch by the roll amount (`steerMix`, `pitchMix`); `aiInputs` applies the inverse so the AI keeps thinking in turn/pitch. Any new input consumer must read the mixed values. *(S14)*
- **`Inputs.pitch` / `Inputs.roll` are optional** (old call sites keep compiling). Smoothed states live in `PodState.pitchCtl / rollCtl`; `p.roll = rollBase·(1-knife) + rollCtl·π/2`. *(S10)*

## Pod contact
- **Pod contact** lives in `physics.ts → collidePods` (game + CI share it). *(S11)*
- Who gets the event of a contact, and which zone a wall hit counts as: `pods.md` → "Damage". *(2026-11-22)*
- **Who pays for a contact (2026-11-25, user: "Gegner nehmen keinen Schaden wenn ich sie ramme, nur ich"):** hull = (closing speed − `RAM_FREE` 5 m/s) × `RAM_RATE` 1, at most `RAM_MAX` 16 (the rammed pod: at most 24 – with a cap of 24 a pod standing still lost 36–43 per pod that ran into it; sim deaths 4 → 6 on 13 tracks, with 16: 0–1), once per pod – shared out so that **the pod that is rammed pays 1.5 ×, the rammer 0.5 ×** (`RAM_SHARE` 0.75; both steering into each other: 1 × each). Who rams is read against the ROAD from the speeds the two arrive with (`collidePods(ps, time, td)`): speed across the road towards the other pod, plus – for the pod behind – the speed it closes in with. It must be computed BEFORE the impulse is applied, and it needs the track: against each other two pods always approach at the same speed (without `td` both pay the same). **The engine on the side of the hit pays** (`applyDamage` with the side; nose or tail: half and half). The same rule for player and Gegner. The event carries `pod` (the other pod) and `dealt` (the hull that pod lost). Unit checks `ram: …` (2). Sim, 13 tracks × 120 s, three seeds: deaths 3 / 4 / 5 before the cap of 16, 1 / 0 / 0 with it. `DEATHS=1` makes the sim print what cost a pod its last six pieces of hull when it dies.

## AI
- **AI start discipline:** `ai.gridLat` (grid lane) is held for ~4.5 s, blended out until 7.5 s; no lateral dodging and lift-off when tailgating during that phase. CI column `startTouch`. *(S16)*
- **AI dive rule** lives in `aiInputs` (`ai.dive`, `ai.diveT`, `ai.diveRoll`); CI fails above 1.5 % dive frames. Do not loosen it without re-measuring. *(S13)*

## Ground under the pod
- **Surfaces are felt (2026-11-23, user decision "deutlich"):** `SURFACES` grip / top speed: ice and ice lake 0.45 · powder 0.78 / 0.86 · snow 0.86 · mud 0.80 / 0.88 · dunes 0.85 / 0.91 · sand, gravel, ash, beach, moon dust 0.90 · rock, built road, deck 1. `WORLD_GRIP` = 1 (`surfaces.ts`): the ice world's ×0.8 and the jungle moon's ×0.9 on top of every surface are gone – "Piste 100 %" was 80 % on Ando Prime. The weather still multiplies (`EnvFx.grip`). **Ice slides on** (`SurfaceDef.slip` 0.6, ice and ice lake only): `grip` is the rate at which sideways speed fades; once a pod slides at more than 3 … 12 m/s sideways that rate drops by a further 60 % – small corrections keep the 45 %, a real slide carries. A unit check that compares two surfaces must swap the surface on ONE stretch of a copied track: banking changes the grip too (`bankGrip`), two different stretches measured 36 % instead of 45 %. The rivals fly all thirteen tracks with these values without a change to their driving (sim: no deaths, no wall contact). Unit checks `ground: …` (2).
- **3D snow (2026-11-23):** fresh powder = `SURFACES.powder.vmax` 0.86; `stepPod` raises top speed and grip towards `TRENCH_VMAX` 0.99 / `TRENCH_GRIP` 0.95 by `p.snowPack` – how much of the snow IN FRONT of the engines is already cut (mean of both banks, read `rad + 1.5 m + one step` ahead of the nose). Reading under the pod would let it profit from its own trench: it cuts at the nose, so everything behind the nose is always cut. `p.snowDepth` = metres of snow standing there (effects only: plume, camera shudder, HUD). The steady speed in fresh powder is ≈ 0.87 of the speed in a trench, not exactly 0.86 / 0.99 (drag is not linear). **Rivals** (`aiInputs`): `snow.trenchNear` gives the trench in reach of where the pod is (middle of a narrow one, 1.5 m inside the nearer wall of a wide lane); it replaces the racing line. `ai.trI` shifts the aim by the lateral error that remains (pure pursuit settles inside a bend – half out of the trench), integrated only when the error is below 3 m (no wind-up on the way over). In the sim eleven rivals cross the field with 50–70 % of the snow ahead already cut on average (`powder:` line of `npm run check`). Details: `strecken.md`.

## Boost and engine heat
- **`stepPod(…, heatMul)`**: the seventh argument is the engine-heat factor of climate and weather (default 1: node sim and unit checks). Heat while boosting = `heatRate × SurfaceDef.heat × heatMul`, cooling = `cool ÷ (SurfaceDef.heat × heatMul)`. Where the numbers come from: `umwelt.md` → „Climate and the boost". *(2026-11-22)*

## Input: keys and gamepad
- **Reversing mirrors the yaw** (`dirF` in `stepPod`): stick right swings the tail to the right, as a car does in reverse. Only the steering term is mirrored – the pull of a damaged engine side (`hpR − hpL`) keeps its direction. The authority runs through 0 at standstill (`lowF`), so nothing jumps. A Gegner never reverses (`aiInputs` only brakes above its target speed). Unit checks `steering: …`. *(2026-11-22)*
- **The game only READS gamepads:** `navigator.getGamepads()` once per frame in `Game.readInput` (so only while a race runs) and in `PadBindings` (settings → Steuerung, as long as that screen is open). It sends nothing to a pad: no vibration, no HID / USB / Bluetooth API. That has been so since the first upload (2026-11-16); Session 16 only made the mapping configurable. **A controller that powers off when a race starts is not switched off by game code** – the race is the first moment the page uses the Gamepad API, and only then does the browser start to poll the pad (in Chromium / Edge on Windows through `Windows.Gaming.Input`). Reported by the user on 2026-11-22 (Edge, wireless Xbox controller: off at every race start, off again 1–2 s after switching it on). Suspected cause, **not confirmed**: Microsoft's GameInput runtime 4.0.300 (October 2026) powered Xbox controllers off by itself, 4.0.302 fixed it (`winget upgrade Microsoft.GameInput`). The sandbox has neither a controller nor Windows; the user's cross-check is an open item in `TODO.md` section 1. Details: `docs/verlauf/2026-11-22-controller-und-action.md`. Do not hunt for this in `readInput` again before that cross-check is in. *(2026-11-22)*
- **Reset key (2026-11-23, user request): action `reset`** – keyboard `Backspace`, gamepad button 8 (Back / Select); rebindable like every action. `manualReset(p)` (physics.ts) takes the pod off the track for `RESET_WAIT` = 0.8 s, then `respawn` puts it at `lastSafePath` / `lastSafeIdx` (centred, along the track, 35 m/s, 2.2 s protection). **It repairs nothing** – hull, lost parts, heat and overdrive stay (`p.manualReset` tells `respawn`); a crash respawn still patches the pod to 55 %. The key rests for `RESET_COOLDOWN` = 3 s and is refused while the pod is off the track or has finished; `Game.resetPlayer` also refuses outside the state `race` and while paused. A reset must never be cheaper than flying on.
- **`tickRespawn(p, td, dt)` is the one place that counts down `p.dead` and puts a pod back** (returns `'patched'` after a crash, `'reset'` after the key). `Game` clears the hit marks only on `'patched'`.
- **A new action needs:** `Action` type, `ACTIONS`, both `KEY_PRESETS`, `defaultPad` – and nothing else: the binding screens list `ACTIONS`. `normalizeKeys` / `normalizePad` give an action that is new to a stored layout its default only where the player has not put that key on something else. Check the „Steuerung" screen with `pui.mjs`: 13 actions are four rows, the screen is full (720 px).
- **Stuck hint:** three seconds below 6 m/s or the wrong way round → the HUD names the reset key once (`Game.watchStuck`).
- **Gamepad:** `PadMap` in `keymap.ts` (`padValue`, `readPadBind`, `bindPad`); `Game.readInput` reads only `this.padmap`; UI `PadBindings`. Start (button 9) = pause, fixed. *(S16)*
- **Keys:** `keymap.ts` is the single source (`ACTIONS`, `KEY_PRESETS`, `normalizeKeys`, `bindKey`). `Game.readInput` / `onKey` only use `this.keymap`; never hard-code `KeyboardEvent.code` again. UI = `ui/KeyBindings.tsx`. New action → add to `Action`, `ACTIONS`, both presets, `readInput`. *(S12)*
- **Key map (`Game.readInput`):** `W` thrust, `S` brake/reverse, `A`/`D` steer, `Shift` boost, `Space` air brake / slide, `↑` dive (`pitch = -1`), `↓` lift (`pitch = +1`), `←`/`→` 90° roll (`roll = ∓1`), `R` repair, `C` view. Arrows never steer. Update `screens.tsx` + `RaceView.tsx` + `README.md` together when changing keys. *(S10)*
