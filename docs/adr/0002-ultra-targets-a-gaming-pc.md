# Ultra targets a gaming PC

The "Ultra" preset may be heavy: it targets the user's gaming PC (Core i9 + RTX 4090), where pods get maximum detail, including full-detail Gegner close to the camera. Lower presets must not get slower than before. The development machine (Dell Latitude 5590, Intel UHD 620) cannot prove Ultra frame rate, so pod budgets are stated in triangles and draw calls, and Ultra is test-driven by the user on the gaming PC (decided 2026-11-21).
